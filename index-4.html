MY CHAPTER AT ZITA

I arrived to learn, build relationships, pursue opportunities and understand the business. I did not know where this chapter would lead. I only knew I wanted to keep moving.

02 / THE EVOLUTION

The title changed.
The responsibility changed.
So did I.

From Business Development Executive to Customer Relationship Manager, the work became about more than closing opportunities. It became about relationships, ownership, coordination and solving what needed to be solved.

03 / ONE LESSON I KEEP
Working is important.
Making your work visible is part of the work too.

If the impact of your work is never seen, even meaningful effort can become invisible. The lesson is not to become louder. It is to communicate your work, your progress and your results. Errors often travel quickly. Your contribution should be visible too.

04 / WHAT I LEARNED
01Do the work.

02Build relationships.
They outlast individual tasks.
03Take ownership.
Even when the problem crosses teams.
04Criticism travels fast.
Let consistent work be visible too.
05Keep learning.
A role is a chapter, not the whole story.

To All Account Managers

You are all pillars, but do not forget what makes a pillar strong.

Work together. Rather than passing pressure down, work with your teams through it. Strong teams are not built by pressure alone. They are built when people stand beside one another.
